## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-tier B2B trade experience engineered specifically for global luxury fashion labels, high-street retail syndicates, and premium department store buyers procuring via OEM/ODM services. It completely bypasses transactional consumer retail tropes—such as aggressive discount stickers, urgency timers, and cluttered promotional carousels—in favor of an architectural, editorial, and industrial presence.

### Visual Aesthetic & Archetype
The style blends **Architectural Minimalism** with **Haute Horlogerie / Editorial Luxury**:
- **Monolithic Discipline:** Deep obsidian backdrops anchored by structural 1px hairline dividers provide an ultra-clean foundation that lets bespoke textile close-ups, fabric draping, and weave patterns lead.
- **Surgical Precision:** Specifications, Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs), yarn counts, testing certifications, and lead times are treated with technical reverence through sharp geometric layouts and tabular layouts.
- **Understated Opulence:** Champagne gold serves strictly as a focused interactive signal and hallmark of craft, never as an ungrounded decorative wash.
- **Buyer Utility:** Rapid conversion via frictionless Request For Quotation (RFQ) drawers, specimen swatch ordering workflows, and clear capacity metrics.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for deep dark mode, creating an immersive gallery aesthetic where high-resolution garment imagery pops with dramatic fidelity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Dark Substrates:**
  - Base Background: `#0D0D0D` (true deep matte obsidian canvas)
  - Surface Primary: `#121212` (standard sectional canvas)
  - Surface Elevated / Card: `#1A1A1A` (card containers, flyout drawers, floating panels)
  - Surface Highlight: `#242424` (hover states, selected table rows)
- **Accents & Precious Metals:**
  - Primary Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`): Primary conversion triggers, key metric highlights, verification badges, active states.
  - Luminous Gold (`#E5C158`): Hover and focused interaction states for buttons and active links.
  - Antique Gold (`#B8972E`): Border highlights, active slider tracks, subtle rule emphasis.
- **Structural Neutrals & Hairlines:**
  - Hairline Border Default: `#2A2A2A` (1px clean separation borders)
  - Hairline Border Subtle: `#1E1E1E` (subordinate grid guides, table cell separators)
  - Hairline Border Focus: `#D4AF37` (focused inputs and active container frames)
- **Text & Editorial Tones:**
  - Text High-Contrast: `#FFFFFF` (Primary headers, critical spec figures, high-impact numbers)
  - Text Standard: `#F5F5F7` (Body content, labels, input typography)
  - Text Muted: `#A1A1AA` (Secondary descriptors, metadata, inactive tab labels)
  - Text Subtle: `#71717A` (Captions, footnotes, table header keys, deactivated controls)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-couture editorial grace with technical manufacturing rigor:
- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers the authority and prestige of high-fashion print monographs. Used for primary collection intros, factory pedigree statements, and hero editorial narratives.
- **Body & Subheads (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Provides pristine legibility across dark backgrounds without visual fatigue. Selected for its balanced geometric proportions and contemporary neutrality.
- **Specifications & Labels (`Space Grotesk`):** Injects engineered precision into technical data sheets, MOQ figures, GSM (grams per square meter) numbers, yarn classifications, and operational badges. Must be displayed with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) when presented in uppercase.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to a strict 12-column architectural grid inspired by modernist trade portfolios. Large expansive margins establish quiet luxury and allow complex manufacturing matrices to breathe.

### Grid Rhythm & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Linear stacked product tiers, sticky bottom action bar for "Request Quotation".
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 2rem`. Two-column specimen layouts with side-by-side technical tables.
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, max-content container width `1440px`, centered with `margin-desktop: 4rem`, `gutter-desktop: 2.5rem`. Allows multi-spec comparison splits and persistent RFQ dossier bars.

### Compositional Rules
- Use generous vertical breathing room (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) between distinct manufacturing categories (e.g., Circular Knitwear vs. Structured Tailoring).
- Dense, highly structured horizontal layouts with tight intra-element padding (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) inside technical data cards to maintain scannability for corporate procurement officers.

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with minimalist architectural principles, the system strictly forbids fuzzy, diffuse drop-shadows or skeuomorphic bevels. Visual depth is established through tonal layering, disciplined borders, and selective glassmorphic frosted plates.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#0D0D0D` — Structural ground of the viewport.
- **Tier 1 (Section Blocks):** `#121212` — Edge-to-edge content bands.
- **Tier 2 (Spec Cards & Containers):** `#1A1A1A` with a crisp `1px solid #2A2A2A` boundary outline.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays / Modals / Swatch Pickers):** Background `#161616` with `85%` opacity combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and a subtle `#D4AF37` hairline stroke (`0.5px`).

### Illumination & State Signifiers
- **Hover Elevation:** Rather than lifting via shadow, hovered cards transition their perimeter border from `#2A2A2A` to `#B8972E` or `#D4AF37` with an ultra-smooth `250ms ease-out` timing curve, accompanied by a subtle internal luminance lift to `#202020`.
- **Focus Rings:** Distinct 1px solid champagne highlight (`#D4AF37`) offset by a 2px `#0D0D0D` moat.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a zero-radius, razor-sharp geometric profile (`roundedness: 0`). 

### Rationale & Rules
- All buttons, product card containers, modal dialogues, input fields, and technical data cells possess crisp `0px` corners.
- This deliberate architectural severity reinforces industrial precision, textile cutting matrices, and bespoke sartorial tailoring.
- Circular elements are solely reserved for 1:1 circular fabric swatch previews, round status pips (e.g., factory capacity indicator), and standard pagination indices.

## Components

### Buttons & RFQ Actions
- **Primary Trade Action (Request Sample / RFQ):**
  - Shape: Sharp `0px` radius.
  - Surface: Solid Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`), Text: Matte Black (`#0D0D0D`), Font: `Space Grotesk`, `label-caps`.
  - Padding: `16px 32px`.
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#E5C158` with a smooth 150ms transition.
- **Secondary Action (Download Tech Pack / Spec Sheet):**
  - Shape: Sharp `0px` radius.
  - Surface: Transparent with `1px solid #A1A1AA`, Text: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Hover: Border color transitions to `#D4AF37`, Text transitions to `#D4AF37`.
- **Tertiary Link:**
  - Understated inline link in `#A1A1AA` with an animated 1px underline that expands from center to edges on hover in `#D4AF37`.

### Spec Cards & Product Matrices
- Encased in `#1A1A1A` with a continuous `1px solid #2A2A2A` border.
- Layout: Top half features 4:5 vertical editorial garment photography; bottom half contains high-density tabular metadata (MOQ, Fabric Composition, GSM, Lead Time, Country of Origin).
- Interactive State: Hover initiates a hairline gold border change (`#B8972E`) and reveals a quick "Add to RFQ Dossier" button.

### Badges, Tags & MOQs
- Sharp rectangular tag containers (`padding: 4px 8px`).
- Style: Monochromatic or accent-bordered (`border: 1px solid #2A2A2A`, `background: #141414`).
- Typography: `Space Grotesk` uppercase, `11px`, letter-spacing `0.12em`.
- Specialized MOQ indicator: Dark charcoal fill with gold numeral highlight (e.g., `MOQ: 500 PCS / STYLE`).

### Form Inputs & Quotation Builders
- Background: `#141414`.
- Border: `1px solid #2A2A2A` default, shifting to `1px solid #D4AF37` upon focus.
- Typography: White text (`#F5F5F7`), placeholder in muted grey (`#71717A`).
- Multi-tier Selectors (e.g., Yarn Grade, Shipping Term Incoterms: FOB, CIF): Flat segmented toggles with sharp borders, selected state marked by an obsidian fill with a gold hairline and text.

### Data Tables (Fabric Specifications & Tolerances)
- Row separators: `1px solid #1E1E1E`.
- Table Header: Uppercase `Space Grotesk`, muted grey (`#71717A`), tracking `0.08em`.
- Data Cells: Aligned on numeric decimals where applicable, utilizing `spec-numeral` styles for instant corporate scanning.
- Hover Row: Highlighted with `#181818` background fill.

### Bespoke B2B Components
- **RFQ Floating Dossier Bar:** A persistent docked panel at the bottom of the screen that tracks selected styles, total estimated volume, and sample counts, featuring an immediate "Finalize Specification Dossier" button.
- **Swatch Spectrum Viewer:** A horizontal swatch selector showing physical lab dips (Pantone references, heather textures) alongside precise fiber percentages (e.g., 95% Mulberry Silk / 5% Elastane).
